### Spider-Man 3 (2007)

**Spider-Man 3** is a superhero film directed by *Sam Raimi* and the final installment in the original Spider-Man trilogy. It follows Peter Parker as he faces multiple threats while struggling with the dark influence of an alien symbiote.

* **Thomas Haden Church** as Flint Marko / Sandman
* **Topher Grace** as Eddie Brock / Venom

## Key Plot Highlights

1. **The Symbiote Suit:** An extraterrestrial black suit bonds with Peter, amplifying his powers—and his aggression.
2. **A Triple Threat:** Spider-Man must deal with his vengeful best friend (New Goblin), a tragic villain (Sandman), and a rival reporter bonded with the discarded symbiote (Venom).
3. **Redemption:** Peter learns the true meaning of forgiveness and responsibility before it's too late.
